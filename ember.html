The Problem

Case managers are drowning in disconnected systems.

One application stores client information.
Another manages documentation.
Another tracks appointments.
Another contains housing resources.
Another handles benefits.

The result is endless clicking, duplicate documentation, forgotten follow-ups, and hours of administrative work that steal time away from clients.

Technology should reduce the workload. Instead, it often creates it.

Our Solution

Ember brings everything together.

Case Management Documentation Treatment Planning Benefits Housing Legal Medical AI Assistance Smart Reminders

Instead of opening ten different systems, everything lives inside one intelligent workspace designed around the actual workflow of behavioral health professionals.

Dashboard

Your entire caseload. One screen.

The Dashboard is the command center of Ember. Rather than searching through menus or multiple applications, staff immediately see what matters most:

  • Active caseload
  • High-risk clients
  • Daily reminders
  • Upcoming deadlines
  • Documentation status
  • Supervisor alerts
  • AI recommendations

Every section is designed to reduce cognitive overload so clinicians know exactly what requires attention next.

Built With AI Where It Actually Helps

Artificial intelligence should remove busy work. Not replace professionals.

Ember uses AI to assist with

  • Clinical documentation

  • Letters

What never changes

Everything remains under clinician control. AI drafts, suggests, and organizes — it never finalizes a clinical decision on its own.
The professional always makes the final decision. Every note, plan, and recommendation is reviewed and approved by the clinician before it becomes part of the record.
Built by Experience

Every workflow exists because it solves a real problem encountered in the field.
